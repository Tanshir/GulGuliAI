Five stages

The five stages, mapped to real estate

Real estate is a long game. The five stages line up with how buyers, sellers and past clients actually behave.

  1. Capture: Portals, open houses, valuation pages

    Every source drops into one pipeline with the property, budget and timeline attached.

  2. Nurture: Months of useful contact

    New-listing alerts and market notes for buyers; neighbourhood updates for would-be sellers.

  3. Close: Viewing to offer to closing

    Track viewings, offers, conditions and closing dates by deal.

  4. Evangelize: Reviews and referrals at closing

    Ask while the keys are still warm, then share the review across profiles.

  5. Reactivate: Anniversaries and home values

    Check in each year with a home-value note and a referral link.

Questions from real estate

Quick answers on speed, sellers, social and staying in touch.

More questions
How fast can I answer a portal lead?

GulGuli can text and email new leads within seconds of arrival, even when you are in a showing. You choose the message and the viewing options.

How do I attract more sellers, not just buyers?

Use a valuation page and a neighbourhood newsletter. People who check their home value are often a year or two from listing, so keep them in a slow, helpful sequence.

Can I post to social without thinking about it daily?

Yes. Plan listings, sold notices and market updates in the social planner a week or a month ahead.

What about clients after closing?

Add them to a past-client list with anniversaries, home-value updates and referral prompts. It runs in the background.

How do I get more reviews?

Ask automatically on the closing date by text or email. Happy clients go to your public profiles; any worries come to you first.
