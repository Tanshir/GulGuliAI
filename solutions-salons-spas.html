  • Reply to DMs and calls fast, even when you are mid-colour.

Salon and spa questions

How do win-backs work?

Pick clients who have not visited in a set time and send a message or offer on a schedule.

Will it cut no-shows?

Reminders and easy rescheduling help a lot, and you can ask for a deposit on longer services.

Can clients book from Instagram?

Yes. Messages arrive in your inbox and you can share your booking link or let the AI assistant offer times.

What should I post?

Before-and-afters, team intros and openings. The social planner lets you schedule a week in one sitting.

When should I ask for reviews?

Within a few hours of the visit usually works well. You can set the delay.
