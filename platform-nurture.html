How it works

From new lead to a human taking over

  1. Lead arrives

    Tagged and placed in your pipeline.

  2. First message

    Sent in seconds, by workflow or AI.

  3. Gentle follow-up

    Nudges at sensible gaps until they book.

  4. You step in

    You get a notification when a reply needs you.

Practical tips

Simple rules for better follow-up

Be quick

A reply in minutes feels attentive. A reply next day feels like an afterthought.

Space it out

A few messages over a week or two is friendly. A daily barrage is not.

Make booking one tap

Every message should end with an easy next step.

Nurture questions

Quick answers.

Can automations stop when someone replies?

Yes. Workflows can pause on reply or booking so people never get a stale message.

Can the AI hand over to me?

Yes. The AI assistant can pass a chat to you when it is unsure or when you want to take over.

Are text reminders included?

Reminders are part of the platform. Message usage is charged as listed under fees.

Do I need to follow texting rules?

Yes. Rules on consent and opt-out vary by country, so check yours. We help you set up opt-out handling.
