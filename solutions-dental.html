A day in the practice

Three small jobs that run themselves

1

Recall reminders

Patients due for a hygiene visit get a friendly text or email, with a link to pick a time. No more working through a printed list.

2

After-hours calls

Missed a call at lunch or after close? A text goes out within seconds, and the AI assistant can offer open appointment slots.

3

Post-visit reviews

A short thank-you and a review link go out after the appointment, so your Google rating grows without awkward asking.

Messaging patients needs their consent. Please talk to us about your compliance needs; we will not make claims on your behalf about privacy or messaging rules.

The five stages

From first search to loyal patient

  1. Capture

    Online booking, a new-patient form and missed-call text-back on your number.

  2. Nurture

    Reminders, pre-visit instructions and answers to common questions.

  3. Close

    Treatment-plan follow-up and estimates sent as a clear link.

  4. Evangelize

    Review requests and a referral nudge for families and friends.

  5. Reactivate

    Gentle outreach to patients who have not been in for a while.

Growth playbook

Five habits that keep a schedule healthy

  • Keep your local listing and website booking link up to date.
  • Reply to every call or message the same day, ideally within minutes.
  • Ask for a review while the visit is fresh.
  • Run separate offers for routine care and larger treatments.
  • Stay in touch between visits so recall feels expected, not random.

Dental questions

Not seeing yours? Ask on live chat.

How do I fill open hygiene slots?

Build a list of patients who are overdue, then send a short message with a booking link. You can also announce a last-minute opening to a waiting list.

Can it reduce no-shows?

Yes. Confirmation and reminder messages go out automatically, and patients can reply to confirm or reschedule.

How do I get more reviews?

A review request is sent after each visit. Replies can be drafted by the AI assistant for you to approve.

Can I promote larger treatments?

Tag patients who showed interest and send them information and a consult link, separate from your routine reminders.

Is GulGuli compliant for healthcare?

That depends on how you use it. Please talk to us about your compliance needs before storing patient information.
