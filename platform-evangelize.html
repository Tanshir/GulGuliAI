Evangelize questions

Short answers to the usual ones.

Which review sites does it work with?

Review requests link to the profile you choose, such as your Google Business Profile or Facebook page. Confirm supported sites with us in the demo.

What about a bad review?

Reply quickly and calmly. GulGuli can send unhappy customers to a private conversation first, so you can fix things before they post.

Can I reuse reviews on my site?

Yes, through website widgets and the social planner.

Should I pay for reviews?

No. Buying reviews breaks the rules of most review sites and erodes trust. Ask real customers instead.
