Accessibility

Built to be usable by everyone

We want every small business owner to be able to use this site, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Our approach

What we do

  • Keyboard first

    Every page works by keyboard alone, with a skip link at the top of each page.

  • Clear structure

    Pages use real headings, labels and landmarks so screen readers can follow them.

  • Readable text

    Body text is sized for reading, with contrast chosen to stay legible in light and dark modes.

If you hit a barrier on this site, tell us what happened and we will fix it.
