Talk to us about Teams

Questions people ask before they start.

Anything else, message us from the chat bubble and a person will answer.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month to month (or yearly if you choose it). Cancel from the billing page at any time and keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens during the 14-day trial?

You get the full plan you chose, plus your first setup call inside the first three days. If it isn't right, cancel before day 14 and you won't be charged.

Does it really work with WhatsApp?

Yes. We connect a WhatsApp Business number to your inbox so your team and the AI can reply from the same thread as SMS, email and Instagram. Broadcasts to WhatsApp use approved templates.

I'm not technical. Will I be able to use it?

That's what the setup calls are for. We build the pipelines, forms and automations with you on screen, then train you on the parts you'll touch daily. Most owners are running on their own by the end of week one.

How does switching from another tool work?

We import contacts, pipelines, templates and booking links on your first setup call, and run both systems side by side until you're ready to switch the old one off. Guides exist for HubSpot, Keap, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Zoho, Pipedrive, Kartra, ClickFunnels, Calendly and more.

Can I bring my own phone number?

Yes. Port your existing business number in, or pick a new local number during setup. Calls, texts and missed-call text-back all run through it.

What does "unlimited users and contacts" mean?

Exactly that. Add your whole team and your whole list without the price changing. Usage charges apply only to what gets sent beyond the included allowances.

Can I white-label it for my own clients?

On the Orchard plan, yes. Your clients see your logo, your domain and your pricing. You manage every account from one dashboard.
