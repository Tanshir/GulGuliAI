About GulGuli

Software that comes with people.

Small businesses deserve the same growth tools as big ones, and someone to help them use those tools. That is the whole idea behind GulGuli.

Our mission

Make growth simple, fair and shared.

Most owners end up paying for six apps that do not talk to each other, then give up on half of them. We put capture, follow-up, closing, reviews and win-back in one place, at one flat price.

And because tools alone do not run a business, every plan comes with people: onboarding calls, live chat and training.

  • One platform instead of a pile of subscriptions
  • Unlimited users and contacts on every plan
  • Humans available when you get stuck

What we value

How we try to work

Five habits we hold ourselves to, in the product and with you.

1

Say it plainly

Clear pricing, clear answers, and no fine print that changes the deal.

2

Reply quickly

A waiting customer is a worried customer. We aim to move fast.

3

Own the outcome

If something is ours to fix, we fix it and tell you what happened.

4

Work as a team

Support, onboarding and product share notes so you only explain things once.

5

Keep learning

Small businesses change fast. So do we, and we listen to what you ask for.

6

Respect your time

Fewer clicks, sensible defaults, and training you can watch in short bites.
