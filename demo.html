Answers before you sign up

The short version. More on the FAQ page.

Will someone help me set up?

Yes. Every plan includes two 1:1 onboarding calls in week one.

Are there extra charges?

Plans are flat monthly prices. Usage such as texts and calls is billed separately; see the fees section on the pricing page.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. There are no contracts.

Can I bring my data from another CRM?

Yes, we help with migration. See switching guides.

Is training ongoing?

Yes: a course and video library, plus office hours every other week.

Can someone do the work for me?

GulGuli Teams provides trained virtual assistants from $149/month. See Teams.
