A simple cadence

Win-back without nagging

Start with a friendly check-in. If there is no reply, follow up with a time-limited offer, then a last gentle note. Space the messages over about 90 days and stop the moment someone answers.

  • Segment before every send
  • Make offers specific, with an end date
  • Include a one-tap booking link

Reactivate questions

Where should I start?

Pick one group, such as customers you have not seen in six months, and send one friendly message.

Can I send the same campaign on several channels?

Yes. Choose the channels in one composer.

How do I avoid unsubscribes?

Send fewer, more relevant messages to smaller segments, and always honour opt-outs.

Are there usage fees for messages?

Some sending has usage fees. See fees on the pricing page.
