Five stages

The five stages, mapped to home services

Home service work follows a clear path from a worried phone call to a repeat customer.

  1. Capture: Calls, texts, forms and ads

    Missed calls, web forms and Facebook messages all land in one inbox as new leads.

  2. Nurture: Follow up on quotes

    Automatic nudges after estimates until they say yes or no.

  3. Close: Book, invoice, get paid

    Calendar slots, deposits and card payment on a link.

  4. Evangelize: Reviews and referrals

    Ask right after the job, when the work is fresh.

  5. Reactivate: Maintenance reminders

    Seasonal services, annual checks and warranty follow-ups bring them back.

Questions from home services

Short answers about missed calls, quotes, reviews and repeat work.

More questions
How do I stop losing jobs when I cannot answer?

Turn on missed-call text-back. The caller gets a message within seconds, and the AI can collect their details and offer a time. For calls, Voice AI can answer for you.

How do I follow up on quotes?

Send the estimate from GulGuli, then let a sequence nudge by text or email after a day or two. You can add a task for yourself if they still do not reply.

How do I get more reviews?

Trigger the request when you mark the job complete, by text, with a simple link. Ask every time.

Can I sell maintenance plans?

Yes. Create recurring invoices, tag plan customers and send reminders before each visit.

Do you have pages for specific trades?

Use the trade guides below for electricians, HVAC, plumbing and cleaning. They share the same platform; workflows are set up for each trade during onboarding.
