What it is

One net for every way people reach you

Lead capture means making it easy to be found and recording who showed up. GulGuli gathers website chats, phone calls, forms and social messages into a single contact list, so nothing lives in someone's pocket or inbox.

C

Chat widget that follows up by text

Visitors chat on your site. If they leave, the conversation moves to text so you do not lose them.

M

Missed-call text-back

Miss a call and the caller gets a friendly text within moments, asking how you can help.

W

Websites, funnels and landing pages

Build pages with forms that feed your CRM directly. Know which page each lead came from.

F

Forms, surveys and quizzes

Ask a few good questions up front, then route each answer to the right follow-up.

How it works

From first touch to first reply

  1. Place capture points

    Add the widget, forms and number routing.

  2. Connect channels

    Link WhatsApp, SMS, email and social accounts.

  3. Leads arrive

    Each is saved with its source and details.

  4. Reply goes out

    An instant message, or the AI assistant, starts the chat.

Good to know

Three habits that win more leads

Reply in minutes

The first business to answer often gets the job. Missed-call text-back makes that automatic.

Offer more than one door

Some people want to call, others prefer a form or a chat. Put all three on your site.

Keep chat going as text

A chat window closes when the tab does. A text thread does not.

Capture questions

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Can I capture leads from social DMs?

Yes. Instagram and Facebook messages arrive in the same inbox as everything else.

Do I need a website already?

No. You can build one in GulGuli, or add forms and chat to a site you have.

What happens when I miss a call?

The caller gets a text from your number, and the conversation appears in your inbox. Voice AI can also pick up on the Bloom and Orchard plans.

Is this on every plan?

Yes. Capture tools are included, with unlimited users and contacts. Usage fees are listed on pricing.
