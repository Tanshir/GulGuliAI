Five stages

The five stages, mapped to entrepreneurs

Every solo business runs the same loop, whether you sell coaching, design, products or courses.

  1. Capture: Be easy to find and easy to reach

    One site, forms and a chat widget that all feed one inbox, so no enquiry sits in a forgotten email.

  2. Nurture: Reply fast, stay friendly

    Automatic first replies, then a short sequence that checks in without sounding like a robot.

  3. Close: Quote, book, get paid

    Send a proposal, take the booking and collect payment through one link.

  4. Evangelize: Turn happy clients into proof

    Ask for a review right after delivery and a referral a little later.

  5. Reactivate: Wake up the quiet list

    Reach past clients and old enquiries when you launch something new.

More questions
How do I follow up if I am always on calls or on the road?

Set up the first reply and a short follow-up sequence once. GulGuli sends them for you, and the mobile app on iOS and Android lets you jump into a conversation from anywhere.

Can I sell courses or memberships on it?

Yes. You can build courses, gate content behind a membership and take payment on the same platform as your CRM.

What should I automate first?

Start with the instant reply to new enquiries and the reminder before a booking. Then add review requests. Those three save the most time.

How do I look established when it is just me?

A clean site, a fast reply, tidy proposals and invoices, and visible reviews. GulGuli covers each of those.

When should I hire a virtual assistant?

When you spend more than a few hours a week on admin that follows a pattern, like data entry, scheduling or inbox triage. GulGuli Teams starts from $149/month.
